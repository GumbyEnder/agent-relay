# Lender-Ready Deal Memo — Maple Court Retail Center (fictional)

Prepared from the offering memorandum, the rent roll and the seller's 2025 operating statement. All figures are USD. Example data: this property, its tenants and its lender do not exist.

## Deal at a glance

The borrower is seeking a $1,690,000 acquisition loan on a 13,500 SF neighborhood retail strip in Springfield, priced at $2,600,000. That is 65.0% loan-to-value on the asking price, a 10.85% debt yield on 2025 net operating income, and a 7.05% cap rate on the ask.

Three of four suites are leased, 81% occupancy, all NNN. The vacant 2,500 SF suite is the single largest source of upside and the single largest source of risk in this file: the in-place income covers the debt, and the vacancy is what the borrower is buying.

## Income and expenses (2025, seller-reported)

Base rent and reimbursements produce $264,100 of effective gross income after a 5% vacancy and credit allowance. Operating expenses of $78,000 plus $2,700 of replacement reserves leave $183,400 of net operating income. The expense ratio is 29.5%, which is normal for a strip centre of this age in this market; the management line of $6,000 is 2.3% of EGI and is the line most likely to move under new ownership.

## Rate stress

The loan amortizes over 25 years. Debt service is computed at the base note rate and at +100bp and +200bp, with the same NOI and the same loan amount.

| Scenario | Note rate | Annual debt service | NOI | DSCR | LTV | Debt yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | 6.75% | $140,117 | $183,400 | 1.31x | 65.00% | 10.85% |
| +100bp | 7.75% | $153,181 | $183,400 | 1.20x | 65.00% | 10.85% |
| +200bp | 8.75% | $166,731 | $183,400 | 1.10x | 65.00% | 10.85% |

At the placeholder minimum DSCR of 1.25x the loan passes at the base rate and fails at +100bp. The failure is a rate-sensitivity finding, not an arithmetic one: the loan size that clears 1.25x at +200bp is roughly $1,556,000, about $134,000 below the request.

## Lease and title issues to clear

- Demo Fitness holds a co-tenancy provision tied to centre occupancy. If occupancy falls further, rent may be reduced; the rent roll does not quantify the reduction and the lease must be read in full before closing.
- Sample Dental holds a right of first refusal to purchase the property. A sale can be delayed while that right is noticed and waived.
- The title commitment shows a recorded access easement across the north parking field in favour of the adjacent parcel, and an open mechanic's lien of $18,400 recorded 2026-08-12 that the seller is to cure.
- The Phase I environmental report is dated 2024 and reports no recognized environmental conditions. It is two years old at closing.

## Comps and indicated value

Three of the five supplied sales are kept: two retail strips within four miles and one neighborhood centre with a similar anchor mix. Two are excluded, one for a sale date outside the 24-month window and one because the reported price includes an adjacent parcel that was not part of the retail improvements. The kept set indicates $2,400,000 to $2,700,000, which brackets the asking price.
