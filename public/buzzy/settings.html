Music

Lets BeeZilla make music from a description. Uses your own ElevenLabs key. The Music API needs a paid ElevenLabs plan (Starter, $6/month) — a free account answers paid_plan_required no matter how good the key is.

Microphone

BeeZilla records a short clip and has it transcribed — the browser's speech engine is never used, so silence here is never a browser quirk. If it cannot hear you, this page says which part failed.
