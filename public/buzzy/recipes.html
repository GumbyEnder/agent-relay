Recipes

A recipe is a way of doing a job that someone has already worked out, tested and sealed. You can see what it does, who made it, when it last changed and what it costs — the method stays sealed until you buy it.

What you always get in public is what a recipe refuses to do. The method — the part someone worked out and paid for with their own mistakes — is only ever opened by the person who owns it. Recipes that touch legal, medical, tax or insurance decisions carry a "stop and see a professional" line, and that line can't be edited out.
