Publish a recipe

You are the author, so you are the seller. Put it on the shelf with a price, or leave it free. The method is sealed the moment you publish — what people see is what it does, never how.

1–120 characters. This is the shelf label.

Up to 600 characters. Write what the buyer receives — if you describe the method, the seal will refuse it, because the description is published in clear.

0 means free. Whole Drops only.

What gets published: your title, description, price, the date, and the checks the recipe ships with. What stays sealed: the instructions themselves — they are encrypted before they are stored, and neither a shopper nor the shelf ever serves them.
